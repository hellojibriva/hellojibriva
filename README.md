# Hi, I'm Abi Precious Jibrin 👋

### One Health & Epidemiology | Disease Surveillance | Public Health Data | Digital Health

I am a public health and One Health professional working at the intersection of **epidemiology, disease surveillance, monitoring & evaluation, health data, and digital health systems**.

### 🐄 Nigeria Livestock Brucellosis Surveillance

**Reproducible epidemiological analysis**

A reproducible analysis of reported livestock brucellosis surveillance data from Nigeria's WOAH WAHIS quantitative dataset.

The analysis deliberately separates **reported surveillance events from disease burden** and documents what the dataset can and cannot support.

**Focus:** Epidemiology · Zoonoses · Brucellosis · One Health · WAHIS · Python · Surveillance Analysis

[View Analysis](https://github.com/hellojibriva/Nigeria-livestock-brucellosis-surveillance)

---

### 📊 Nigeria Brucellosis WAHIS Dashboard

**Interactive livestock disease surveillance intelligence**

An interactive dashboard translating the WAHIS brucellosis surveillance extract into reporting coverage, temporal patterns, geographic distribution, species/category analysis, data-quality assessment, and record-level exploration.

**Focus:** Disease Surveillance · Epidemiology · Data Visualization · One Health · React · TypeScript · WAHIS

[View Dashboard](https://github.com/hellojibriva/Nigeria-brucellosis-WAHIS-dashboard) · [Live Demo](https://hellojibriva.github.io/Nigeria-brucellosis-WAHIS-dashboard/)
