Method

Indicators are summed across facility-month reports and never averaged. Rates are ratios of sums: ANC4 retention = ANC4 / ANC1, IPTp completion = IPT3 / IPT1, positivity = positive / tested, treatment = treated / positive, FP acceptance = acceptors / counselled.

Privacy

Facility names are replaced by random codes within zone, funding partners by letters, and state, LGA and ward are removed. Free-text fields are dropped. Totals match the source workbook exactly.
